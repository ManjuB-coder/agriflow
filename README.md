# 🌾 AgriFlow AI

## Smart Farming Assistant Powered by AI

AgriFlow AI is an AI-powered smart farming assistant designed to help farmers make informed decisions through disease detection, weather intelligence, crop information, market insights, government schemes, and AI-based assistance.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Farmers often rely on multiple platforms for crop disease diagnosis, weather updates, market prices, farming guidance, and government schemes.

This fragmented access to information makes decision-making difficult and time-consuming.

AgriFlow AI solves this problem by bringing all essential agricultural services into a single, easy-to-use platform.
